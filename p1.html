Unsaid

by Rish

Beneath the weight of unsaid words,
silence hums like a taut string,
vibrating with what we dare not speak.

It lingers, not as a bridge or a barrier,
but a fragile thread on the verge of snapping.

If I spoke, would the air shatter?
Would truth bloom,
or scatter into echoes
only the heart remembers?
