Liberation

“No bars should dull its fiery flames.”

Through verdant veils, it soars unbound,
A hymn of freedom, a sacred sound.
Amber and sapphire, skies it claims,
No bars should dull its fiery flames.
The bough extends, a cradle, not a chain,
Let wings find skies, not sorrow’s domain.
