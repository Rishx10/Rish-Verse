Crypt Rules

Decipher the hidden 5-letter poetry fragment in 6 attempts. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.

After each submission, the tiles will shift colors to reveal how close your alignment is to the verse:

Visual Clues
W
Sage Green: Perfect. Letter is correct and in the right spot.
O
Muted Gold: Misplaced. In the word, but wrong spot.
R
Charcoal: Missing. Not in the secret word at all.
